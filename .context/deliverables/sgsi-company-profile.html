02 Who We Are Tentang Kami 关于我们

In mining and commodity trading, every decision depends on data. The accuracy of a single test result can decide whether a cargo is accepted, disputed, or rejected.

Calorific value, moisture, ash, sulfur, sample condition, and chain-of-custody records all directly affect commercial value, operational planning, shipment acceptance, and buyer confidence.

PT Sentra Geo Survey Indonesia (SGSI) is an independent coal testing laboratory established to serve exactly this need. Based in Samarinda, East Kalimantan — at the heart of one of Indonesia's most active mining regions — and established in 2026, SGSI provides accurate, timely, and independent coal testing to producers, traders, and end-buyers serving the thermal and coking coal markets.

Our core service portfolio

  • TMTotal Moisture — basis for as-received calculations and contract weight adjustments.
  • ProximateInherent Moisture, Ash Content, Volatile Matter, Fixed Carbon.
  • GCV / NCVCalorific Value — the primary parameter for energy content and commercial quality.
  • Total STotal Sulfur — quality specification, environmental requirements, and contract terms.
  • G-indexCaking Index (GB/T 5447) — for coking coal evaluation.

Every parameter is determined using internationally recognised test methods drawn from ASTM, ISO, and GB/T, ensuring that our results are presented in the formats expected by buyers across Indonesia, China, India, and the wider Asian market. Our facility, equipment selection, procedures, and quality management system are being developed with reference to the requirements of SNI ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — the Indonesian adoption of the international standard for testing laboratory competence — with KAN accreditation as a defined strategic objective.

04 Our Services Layanan Kami 我们的服务

Coal Laboratory Testing

SGSI specialises in the commercial-grade coal parameters that drive contracts, royalty calculations, and end-user procurement decisions across the thermal and coking coal markets.

TM · ASTM / ISO

Total Moisture

Determination of total moisture in as-received coal samples; the basis for as-received calorific value calculations and, where the contract provides for it, weight adjustment.

Proximate · ASTM / ISO

Proximate Analysis

Complete four-component breakdown: Inherent Moisture, Ash Content, Volatile Matter, and Fixed Carbon. The standard quality envelope for any thermal coal contract.

GCV · NCV · Bomb Calorimetry

Calorific Value

Gross and net calorific value determination by bomb calorimetry; the primary parameter for evaluating energy content and commercial quality, read alongside moisture, ash, and sulfur. Results reported in kcal/kg, MJ/kg, or BTU/lb as required.

Sulfur · IR Detection

Total Sulfur

High-temperature combustion infrared determination; a parameter for quality specification, environmental requirements, equipment corrosion management, and contractual penalty terms.

G-index · GB/T 5447

Caking Index (G-index)

Determination per GB/T 5447 for evaluating the coking behaviour of bituminous coals; particularly relevant for producers and traders serving the Chinese metallurgical sector.

Sample Preparation

Reliable laboratory results begin with proper sample preparation. SGSI operates a dedicated preparation area — fully separated from the analytical laboratory — for crushing, sample division, drying, and milling of incoming coal samples. Preparation is included as part of every analysis we perform, and may also be offered as a standalone service for clients who require professional preparation ahead of analysis at a third-party laboratory.

Looking Ahead

As SGSI grows, our development roadmap includes the expansion of analytical capability beyond coal into mineral assay for exploration and trading clients, and the build-out of field sampling services — including stockpile, barge, and jetty sampling support — to deliver a more integrated technical service for the mining industry. These capabilities will be introduced progressively as equipment, personnel, and accreditation scope allow.

Pengujian Laboratorium Batubara

SGSI mengkhususkan diri pada parameter-parameter batubara bermutu komersial yang menjadi dasar kontrak, perhitungan royalti, dan keputusan pengadaan oleh end-user di pasar batubara termal maupun kokas.

TM · ASTM / ISO

Total Moisture

Penentuan kadar Total Moisture pada sampel batubara as-received; dasar bagi perhitungan nilai kalori as-received dan, sesuai ketentuan kontrak, penyesuaian berat.

Proksimat · ASTM / ISO

Analisis Proksimat

Penguraian lengkap empat komponen: Inherent Moisture, Ash Content, Volatile Matter, dan Fixed Carbon. Cakupan mutu standar bagi setiap kontrak batubara termal.

GCV · NCV · Bomb Calorimetry

Nilai Kalori

Penentuan Gross dan Net Calorific Value menggunakan bomb calorimetry; parameter utama untuk evaluasi kandungan energi dan kualitas komersial batubara, dibaca bersama moisture, ash, dan sulfur. Hasil dilaporkan dalam kcal/kg, MJ/kg, atau BTU/lb sesuai permintaan.

Sulfur · Deteksi IR

Total Sulfur

Penentuan menggunakan metode pembakaran suhu tinggi dengan deteksi infra-merah; parameter untuk evaluasi spesifikasi kualitas, persyaratan lingkungan, manajemen korosi peralatan, dan ketentuan penalti kontraktual.

G-index · GB/T 5447

Caking Index (G-index)

Penentuan sesuai GB/T 5447 untuk mengevaluasi sifat pengkokasan batubara bituminus; khususnya relevan bagi produsen dan trader yang melayani sektor metalurgi Tiongkok.

Preparasi Sampel

Hasil laboratorium yang andal berawal dari preparasi sampel yang tepat. SGSI mengoperasikan area preparasi khusus — terpisah sepenuhnya dari laboratorium analitik — untuk crushing, pembagian sampel, pengeringan, dan milling sampel batubara yang masuk. Preparasi merupakan bagian dari setiap analisa yang kami lakukan, dan juga dapat ditawarkan sebagai layanan tersendiri bagi klien yang membutuhkan preparasi profesional sebelum analisa di laboratorium pihak ketiga.

Pengembangan Ke Depan

Seiring pertumbuhan SGSI, peta jalan pengembangan kami mencakup perluasan kapabilitas analitik di luar batubara menuju analisa mineral untuk klien eksplorasi dan perdagangan, serta pembangunan layanan field sampling — termasuk dukungan sampling stockpile, tongkang, dan jetty — untuk menghadirkan layanan teknis yang lebih terintegrasi bagi industri pertambangan. Kapabilitas ini akan diperkenalkan secara bertahap sejalan dengan kesiapan peralatan, personel, dan cakupan akreditasi.

煤炭实验室检测

SGSI 专注于决定商业合同、矿业权益金计算及终端用户采购决策的商业级煤炭参数 — 涵盖动力煤与炼焦煤两大市场。

TM · ASTM / ISO

全水分(Total Moisture)

收到基煤样的全水分测定;是收到基发热量计算的基础,并按合同约定用于重量调整。

Proximate · ASTM / ISO

工业分析

完整的四组分分析:内在水分、灰分、挥发分及固定碳。是任何动力煤合同的标准质量框架。

GCV · NCV · 氧弹量热

发热量(GCV / NCV)

使用氧弹式量热仪测定高位与低位发热量;评价煤炭能量含量与商业品质的主要参数,需与水分、灰分及硫分共同判读。结果可按客户要求以 kcal/kg、MJ/kg 或 BTU/lb 报告。

Sulfur · 红外检测

全硫(Total Sulfur)

采用高温燃烧红外吸收法测定;用于质量规格评价、环保要求、设备腐蚀管理及合同惩罚条款。

G-index · GB/T 5447

粘结指数(G-index)

依据 GB/T 5447 测定,用于评估烟煤的粘结性能;尤其适用于服务中国冶金行业的生产商与贸易商。

样品制备

可靠的实验室结果始于规范的样品制备。SGSI 设有专属的样品制备区 — 与分析实验室完全隔离 — 用于煤样的破碎、缩分、干燥与研磨。样品制备是我们每次分析工作的组成部分,也可作为独立服务提供给需要在第三方实验室进行后续分析、但希望由专业机构完成前期制备的客户。

未来发展

随着 SGSI 的成长,我们的发展规划包括将分析能力从煤炭领域拓展至 矿产化验,以服务勘探与贸易客户;同时建设 现场采样服务 — 包括煤堆、驳船及码头采样支持 — 为矿业行业提供更具整合性的技术服务。这些能力将随着设备、人员及认证范围的具备而逐步推出。

06 Facility & Workflow Fasilitas & Alur Kerja 设施与工作流

SGSI operates from a purpose-designed laboratory facility in Samarinda, structured to enforce strict separation between dirty preparation work and clean analytical work.

This separation is not a convenience — it is a deliberate contamination-control measure, fundamental to producing reliable results.

BBasement

Dirty Sample Preparation

Raw sample receiving, crushing, jaw and roll milling, sample division, drying, retained sample storage, and B3 waste handling. Direct external access via a dedicated ramp enables sample intake and waste collection without entering the clean laboratory upstairs.

L1Level One

Clean Laboratory, Reception & Administration

The official client-facing level: lobby and reception, sample registration, the clean analytical laboratory (calorimetry, sulfur, proximate, caking index, analytical weighing), QA review, reporting office, and gas storage. Only sealed, prepared analytical sub-samples enter this level.

Our Process

A coal test result is only as reliable as the process that produced it. Every sample at SGSI moves through a controlled, documented workflow from intake to report.

  1. Sample Reception & Registration

    Weighed, logged, and assigned a unique sample ID against client, origin, contract reference, and requested parameters.

  2. Controlled Transfer

    Raw samples move directly from reception to the basement preparation area through a controlled route, never crossing the clean laboratory.

  3. Sample Preparation

    Crushing, rotary sample division for homogeneity, drying, and milling to the particle size required by each test method.

  4. Air-Dried Loss & Conditioning

    Prepared samples equilibrated to laboratory conditions; moisture loss recorded as the ADL value.

  5. Analytical Determination

    Sealed prepared sub-samples analysed on dedicated instrument stations against the relevant ASTM, ISO, or GB/T method.

  6. Data Review & Reporting

    Results reviewed against internal acceptance criteria, calculated to the requested reporting basis (as-received, air-dried, dry, or dry ash-free), and issued in a formal test report citing method, result, unit, basis, date, and analyst.

  7. Sample Retention

    A reserve sub-sample is set aside from the prepared analytical portion before testing begins, sealed in a labelled airtight container, and stored in controlled conditions for the period agreed with the client. Available for re-analysis, umpire testing, or referee resolution in the event of a result dispute.

SGSI beroperasi dari fasilitas laboratorium yang dirancang khusus di Samarinda, ditata untuk menegakkan pemisahan tegas antara pekerjaan preparasi yang berdebu dan pekerjaan analitik yang bersih.

Pemisahan ini bukanlah sekadar kenyamanan — melainkan desain pengendalian kontaminasi yang disengaja, fundamental untuk memperoleh hasil yang andal.

Alur Kerja Kami

Hasil uji batubara hanya seandal alur kerja yang menghasilkannya. Setiap sampel di SGSI melewati alur kerja yang terkendali dan terdokumentasi sejak penerimaan hingga penerbitan laporan.

  1. Penerimaan & Registrasi Sampel

    Ditimbang, dicatat, dan diberi ID sampel unik yang terhubung dengan informasi klien, asal, referensi kontrak, dan parameter yang diminta.

  2. Transfer Terkendali

    Sampel mentah berpindah langsung dari area penerimaan ke area preparasi di basement melalui rute yang terkendali, tanpa melewati laboratorium bersih.

  3. Preparasi Sampel

    Crushing, pembagian sampel menggunakan Rotary Sample Divider untuk homogenitas, pengeringan, dan milling hingga ukuran partikel yang dipersyaratkan masing-masing metode uji.

  4. Air-Dried Loss & Pengkondisian

    Sampel yang telah dipreparasi diseimbangkan dengan kondisi laboratorium; kehilangan kadar air dicatat sebagai nilai ADL.

  5. Penetapan Analitik

    Sub-sampel yang telah dipreparasi dan tersegel dianalisa pada stasiun-stasiun instrumen khusus sesuai metode ASTM, ISO, atau GB/T yang relevan.

  6. Review Data & Pelaporan

    Hasil ditinjau terhadap kriteria penerimaan internal, dihitung ke basis pelaporan yang diminta (as-received, air-dried, dry, atau dry ash-free), dan diterbitkan dalam laporan pengujian resmi yang mencantumkan metode, hasil, satuan, basis, tanggal, dan analis.

  7. Penyimpanan Cadangan Sampel

    Sebuah sub-sampel cadangan diambil dari porsi analitik yang telah dipreparasi sebelum pengujian dimulai, disegel dalam wadah kedap udara yang berlabel, dan disimpan dalam kondisi terkendali untuk jangka waktu yang disepakati dengan klien. Tersedia untuk analisa ulang, umpire testing, atau penyelesaian referee dalam hal terjadi sengketa hasil.

SGSI 在三马林达运营一座专门设计的实验室设施,在布局上确保粉尘制备工作与洁净分析工作之间的严格分离。

这种分离不是出于便利 — 而是有意为之的污染控制设计,是产出可靠结果的根本前提。

我们的流程

煤炭检测结果的可靠程度,取决于其背后的工作流。SGSI 的每一份样品,从接收到报告均经过一套受控且文件化的工作流。

  1. 样品接收与登记

    称重、登记,并赋予唯一样品 ID,与客户信息、样品来源、合同编号及所申请的参数相关联。

  2. 受控转运

    原始样品由接收区直接转运至地下层制备区,沿受控路径移动,不穿越洁净实验室。

  3. 样品制备

    破碎、使用旋转分样器(Rotary Sample Divider)进行缩分以确保均匀性、干燥,以及研磨至各检测方法所要求的粒度。

  4. 空气干燥失重与平衡

    制备完成的样品与实验室空气干燥条件平衡;水分损失记录为 ADL 值。

  5. 分析测定

    已密封的分析子样品在专用仪器工位上,按相应的 ASTM、ISO 或 GB/T 方法进行测定。

  6. 数据复核与报告

    结果对照内部接收标准进行复核、按客户要求的报告基(收到基、空气干燥基、干基、干燥无灰基)进行换算,并出具正式检测报告,注明方法、结果、单位、基准、日期与分析员。

  7. 留样保存

    在测试开始之前,从已制备的分析样品中预先取出一份留样,密封于贴有标签的密闭容器中,在受控条件下储存至与客户约定的期限。可用于在结果争议发生时进行复测、仲裁化验或裁决性复验。
